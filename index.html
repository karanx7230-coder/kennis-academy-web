FAQ

Questions Parents Actually Ask.

Straight answers — no fine print.

Which classes and boards do you cover?
All classes from 1st to 12th — CBSE, PSEB and ICSE boards. For +1 and +2 we cover all four streams: Arts, Commerce, Medical and Non-Medical. Classes 1–10 get all subjects, with a dedicated Maths focus for Classes 5–10.
Which government exams do you prepare students for?
SSC, Banking, Railway (RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician), Defence / Indian Army exams, and Punjab government posts including Punjab Police, Clerk and Patwari.
What is the home tuition fee?
₹400 per hour — transparent, with no hidden charges. We offer dedicated Mathematics home tuition for Classes 5–10, and other classes/subjects on request.
What are the monthly batch fees?
Our fees are deliberately affordable — they vary by class and course. Call 88729-81296 or WhatsApp us for the exact fee for your child's class. You'll get a clear answer, not a sales pitch.
What are the batch timings?
Timings vary by class and course, with morning and evening options. Call 88729-81296 for the current timetable and we'll find a slot that fits your schedule.
Where exactly is the academy?
At Shalki Academy, near Ramleela Ground, Ropar (Rupnagar), Punjab — a landmark everyone in Ropar knows. See the map below for directions.
How do we enrol?
Simple: call 88729-81296 / 99143-09166 or message us on WhatsApp. Better yet — visit the centre, meet Kuljeet sir, see the classrooms, and then decide. No pressure.
